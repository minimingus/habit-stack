Current Flow — Swipe Cards

The FAB opens a full-screen modal with Tinder-style cards. Tapping "Adopt" triggers a second half-sheet to pick a time — two full steps of friction just to add one habit. The swipe metaphor is novel but adds no real benefit over a simple list.

Issues Identified
✗ 2-step friction — swipe to choose, then a second sheet to confirm the time of day
✗ No scan mode — can only see one habit at a time, no way to compare options
✗ Skip is destructive — swiped cards disappear from the stack with no recovery
✗ Unfamiliar gesture — habit apps don't commonly use swipe-to-decide; increases learning curve
✗ Low information density — one card fills the whole screen; wastes real estate
What Works
✓ Single focus — one habit at a time reduces initial overwhelm for new users
✓ Rich description — card has ample room for explanation text and context
